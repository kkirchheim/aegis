📋 Evaluation Aspects

15 reproducibility criteria across three tiers:

Critical (5)

Results reproducibility, dependencies, environment, hyperparameters, dataset availability

High Value (4)

Configuration, tests, documentation, randomness control

Nice-to-Have (6)

CI/CD, license, data versioning, version compatibility
